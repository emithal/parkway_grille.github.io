OUR CHEF

Chef Justin Webb has called the Pacific Northwest his home, since birth, born and raised in Portland, OR. Chef Justin discovered his passion for food as a child collecting his grandmother's recipes. His career began at Portland icons such as Elephant' Delicatessen and Papa Hadyn, in the early 2000's.This is where he quickly learned the fundamentals of cooking, gaining a deeper love of the craft. His focus is on seasonal, vibrant flavors and striving to bring happy people together. Chef Justin has called Wilsonville his home since 2019, with his wife Jordin and family.
